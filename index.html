selected work

Problems worth writing down

decryption

SSL Forward Proxy rollout

Encrypted traffic was crossing the perimeter uninspected, so threat prevention only saw metadata. Introducing decryption at scale risked breaking pinned certificates and proxied SaaS.

Built a decryption CA and a four-rule policy architecture — explicit no-decrypt for regulated categories, dynamic bypass for cloud proxy nodes via external dynamic lists, inspection everywhere else. Piloted first, then tuned exclusions against real failure data.

Inspection extended across the estate with no sustained application breakage.
zero trust migration

GlobalProtect to Zscaler ZPA

A global insurer needed 2,000+ users moved off a full-tunnel VPN model onto application-level access, with no flag day and no disruption to regional users on web proxy only.

Ran application discovery to find what people actually reached over the tunnel, designed app segments around those flows, and cut over in phased cohorts with rollback at each stage.

2,000+ users migrated; per-application access replaced full network exposure.
device trust

Certificate-based VPN authentication

Credentials and MFA proved who was connecting but said nothing about what from — a valid login on an unmanaged laptop looked identical to a corporate asset.

Added a machine certificate as an authentication factor on the GlobalProtect portal and gateway, and coordinated issuance so the rollout stayed invisible to end users.

Managed-device posture became an enforced condition of access.
observability

Rebuilding the log pipeline

Duplicate ingestion paths were sending the same events into the SIEM more than once, inflating volume and putting audit-critical telemetry at risk of being dropped.

Traced every source and forwarding path, rebuilt the syslog-ng layer with one authoritative route per source, and validated end to end before decommissioning the redundant paths.

Duplicate ingestion eliminated; SIEM data quality restored.
